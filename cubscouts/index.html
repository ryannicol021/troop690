About Pack 690

Pack 690 introduces young people and their families to the values of Scouting through fun adventures and hands-on learning. Cub Scouts build confidence, make lifelong friends, and begin a journey guided by the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

🎉

Fun & Friendship

Enjoy games, activities, crafts, the Pinewood Derby, seasonal events, and plenty of opportunities to have fun with friends while learning new skills.

🎖️

Adventure & Advancement

Cub Scouts earn badges through hands-on adventures that build confidence, encourage curiosity, and introduce skills they'll use throughout their Scouting journey.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Scouting

Cub Scouting is a family program where parents, guardians, and siblings are encouraged to participate, creating shared experiences and supporting each Scout's growth.

🧭

Path to Troop 690

Pack 690 prepares Cub Scouts for the next step in Scouting, building confidence, character, and friendships before crossing over into Scouting America Troop 690.

Interested in Joining?

Pack 690 welcomes boys in kindergarten through fifth grade and their families to discover the fun of Cub Scouting. Whether you're new to Scouting or continuing your journey, we'd love to meet you.
