About Troop 690

Since 1962, Troop 690 has provided generations of Scouts with opportunities to grow as leaders, serve their community, explore the outdoors, and live by the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

🏕️

Outdoor Adventure

Monthly camping trips, hiking, backpacking, canoeing, pioneering, and high adventure experiences.

⚜️

Youth Leadership

Troop 690 is youth-led, allowing Scouts to plan, organize, and lead the troop with guidance from experienced adult leaders.

🤝

Service

Scouts strengthen our parish and community through meaningful service projects throughout the year.

🦅

Eagle Scout Tradition

Troop 690 has proudly produced 148 Eagle Scouts who continue to lead and serve their communities.

The Scout Oath

On my honor I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.
