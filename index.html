About Troop 690

Since 1962, Troop 690 has provided generations of Scouts with opportunities to grow as leaders, serve their community, explore the outdoors, and live by the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

🏕️

Outdoor Adventure

Monthly camping trips, hiking, backpacking, canoeing, pioneering, and high adventure experiences.

⚜️

Youth Leadership

Troop 690 is youth-led, allowing Scouts to plan, organize, and lead the troop with guidance from experienced adult leaders.

🤝

Service

Scouts strengthen our parish and community through meaningful service projects throughout the year.

🦅

Eagle Scout Tradition

Troop 690 has proudly produced 148 Eagle Scouts who continue to lead and serve their communities.

The Scout Oath

On my honor I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.

Our History

More than six decades of Scouting, leadership, and service.

2026

Theodore Roosevelt Council and Suffolk County Council merge to become Scouting America Long Island.

2025

Boy Scouts of America becomes Scouting America.

Joseph Caniano is named Scoutmaster.

2022

Troop 239, originally chartered by the Seaford American Legion, merges with Troop 690.

2020

Ryan Kreuder is named Scoutmaster.

2019

Rev. Joseph Fitzgerald is named the fifth Executive Officer.

2017

Theodore Roosevelt Council celebrates its centennial.

Eric Quinn becomes Troop 690's 100th Eagle Scout.

James Harinski is named Scoutmaster.

2012

Troop 690 celebrates its 50th anniversary.

2010

Boy Scouts of America celebrates its centennial.

2009

Edward Lettieri is named Scoutmaster.

Rev. Robert Hayden is named the fourth Executive Officer.

2007

Ryan Woodward becomes Troop 690's 50th Eagle Scout.

2000

Rev. William Koenig is named the third Executive Officer.

1997

Nassau County Council becomes Theodore Roosevelt Council.

1992

Nassau County Council celebrates its 75th anniversary.

1987

Troop 690 celebrates its 25th anniversary.

1985

Boy Scouts of America celebrates its 75th anniversary.

1971

Rev. Thomas Leavey is named the second Executive Officer.

1967

Nassau County Council celebrates its 50th anniversary.

Michael O'Moore becomes Troop 690's first Eagle Scout.

1962

Troop 690 is founded in Seaford, New York and chartered by St. William the Abbot Roman Catholic Church.

Rev. Msgr. Dermod Flinn is named the first Executive Officer.

1960

Boy Scouts of America celebrates its 50th anniversary.

1942

Nassau County Council celebrates its 25th anniversary.

1935

Boy Scouts of America celebrates its 25th anniversary.

1927

St. William the Abbot Roman Catholic Church is officially established, later becoming the chartered organization of Troop 690.

1917

Theodore Roosevelt Council is founded as Nassau County Council.

1912

Arthur Eldred of Rockville Centre Troop 1 becomes America's first Eagle Scout.

1910

Scouting America is founded as the Boy Scouts of America.
