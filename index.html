Who We Are

For more than six decades, Troop 690 has helped young people develop leadership, character, citizenship, and outdoor skills through the Scouting program.

Chartered by St. William the Abbot Roman Catholic Church in Seaford, New York, Troop 690 is a youth-led troop where Scouts grow through camping, service, advancement, and leadership.

Whether hiking a trail, serving the community, earning merit badges, or mentoring younger Scouts, our members challenge themselves to live by the ideals of the Scout Oath and Scout Law every day.

What We Do

Outdoor Adventure

Camp year-round, hike, backpack, canoe, and develop confidence through outdoor experiences.

Leadership

Youth leaders plan meetings, organize campouts, and guide the troop through servant leadership.

Service

Give back to our church, community, and nation through meaningful service projects throughout the year.

Advancement

Progress from Scout to Eagle while building lifelong skills, confidence, and friendships along the way.

Scout Oath

On my honor I will do my best

To do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law;

To help other people at all times;

To keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.

Scout Law

A Scout is

Trustworthy Loyal Helpful Friendly Courteous Kind Obedient Cheerful Thrifty Brave Clean Reverent

Our History
