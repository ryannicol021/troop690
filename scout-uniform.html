Scout Uniform

The Scout uniform is a symbol of character and unity. Wearing it correctly reflects the values of Scouting while showing respect for the troop, the movement, and one another.

Class A Uniform

The Class A uniform, officially known as the field uniform, is worn for troop meetings, Courts of Honor, travel, Scout Sunday, and other formal Scouting events.

• Official uniform shirt with insignia.
• Scout shorts or pants with belt.
• Troop neckerchief and slide.
• Closed-toe shoes.

Class B Uniform

The Class B uniform is worn during many outdoor activities, summer camp, service projects, and other occasions when the full uniform is not required.

Troop 690 offers custom troop T-shirts and hooded sweatshirts each year, providing a comfortable way to represent the troop while participating in Scouting activities.
